## Book preview sound

`scripts/pi/portal/assets/storytime/page-turn.ogg` is a short excerpt of
[Turning a page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turning_a_page.ogg)
by planish, originally distributed through PDSounds and released by its author
into the public domain. The recording is trimmed to 1.15 seconds, converted to
mono, and faded at both ends. Native previews play it quietly through the
existing family decoder; rapid navigation stops it immediately.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 4 October 2026.
The book geometry, personalised lettering, illustration and movement remain
native QML/CSS; these images provide only photographed surface materials.

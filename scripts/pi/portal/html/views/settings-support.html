How do I add a programme?

Open a channel in Your TV, tap its add button, select your files and upload. Track preparation in Media management.

How do I open the parent menu on TV?

Hold Back / Previous for 3.5 seconds, then press OK three times.

What if the picture freezes?

Restart the TV player below. If it happens again, download a support bundle straight afterwards.

Why is a phone video taking a while?

Phone videos may need conversion to a Pi-friendly copy. Check their progress in Uploads and optimisation.

Restart and power

Each action asks for confirmation. Restarting interrupts TV playback; shutting down makes the portal unavailable.

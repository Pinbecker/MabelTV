Connect this TV

Enter the six-digit setup code shown on the Mabel TV screen or at the end of installation. It proves you are beside the Raspberry Pi.

Choose your parent PIN

This locks the grown-up browser dashboard. The television’s physical adult shortcut is separate, so children cannot stumble into it while watching. Use 4–8 numbers they will not guess.

Start with a few channels

You can rename, add, or remove channels later. “Fill screen” gives the familiar edge-to-edge TV look; “Show whole picture” keeps every part of the video visible.

Forgot the parent PIN?

Shut down the Raspberry Pi, connect its boot microSD card or USB drive to a computer, and create an empty file named mabeltv-reset-pin on the visible boot drive. Put it back and start Mabel TV. A new setup code will appear; your videos and channels stay untouched.

Everyday help

Using Mabel TV

How do I add a programme?

Open Add media, choose its channel, choose a video, then tap Upload. It appears on the TV as soon as publishing finishes.

How do I open the grown-up menu on the TV?

Hold Back / Previous for 3.5 seconds, then press OK three times. This physical shortcut is separate from the browser PIN. The on-TV menu controls the CRT look, sound, remote lock, and playback settings.

Which remote can I use?

A USB keyboard or keyboard-style USB remote works immediately. The keys are listed in the owner guide. A GPIO infrared receiver and almost any IR remote can also be added, but it must be wired and learned using the optional IR setup guide.

What if the picture freezes?

First use Restart TV player above. If it happens again, download a support bundle immediately; it preserves the temperature, power, service, and crash evidence needed to identify the cause.

Why is a phone video taking a while?

Videos over 30 fps and large iPhone MOV files are converted to a Pi-friendly 720p/30 fps copy. Mabel TV pauses conversion if the Pi gets too hot, then resumes automatically after it cools.

Shut down the Raspberry Pi, put its boot card or USB drive in a computer, and create an empty file called mabeltv-reset-pin on the visible boot drive. Put it back and start your TV. Your videos and channels will stay untouched.

Everyday help

Using your TV

How do I add a programme?

Open Add videos, choose its channel, choose one or more files, then upload.

How do I open the grown-up menu on TV?

Hold Back / Previous for 3.5 seconds, then press OK three times.

What if the picture freezes?

Use Restart TV player. If it happens again, download a support bundle immediately.

Why is a phone video taking a while?

Large or high-frame-rate iPhone MOV files are converted to a Pi-friendly copy. Normal MP4 programmes are left untouched.

Power

Restart or shut down safely

Use these controls instead of pulling the cable. A safe shutdown protects the SD card.
